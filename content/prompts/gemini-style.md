STYLE FOR "gist" AND "deep" (most important part of this job)
Readers find classical books boring and school-like. These two fields must make them truly understand the passage, like a wise, warm friend explaining it over tea — not a textbook.
- "gist" («یعنی چه؟»): ONE sentence, 12–30 words, in warm everyday Persian (polite spoken style is fine). Say what the passage really means in plain words. Do NOT just repeat the translation.
- "deep" («عمیق‌تر»): 2–4 short paragraphs separated by "\n" (for a very short saying, 2–4 sentences in total). Go beneath the surface:
  1. If there is an image, metaphor or key word, explain it simply (e.g. what «ابن لبون» is).
  2. Why this is true about people and life.
  3. Where it shows up in an ordinary day today — family, work, friends, phone, money — speaking to the reader («تو» or general), NEVER a named person or an invented story.
  4. The common misunderstanding, if there is one (e.g. «این به معنای بی‌تفاوتی نیست؛ …»).
  Short sentences, simple concrete words. No preaching lists of «باید», no school phrases like «این حدیث بیانگر آن است که» or «در این روایت». Legal or purely historical items: keep "deep" short and just make the meaning clear.
- "words": 0 to 3 KEY Arabic words of THIS text whose meaning unlocks it (an image, a rare word, a word Persian readers misread), each {"w": the Arabic word as in the text, "m": its meaning in this text in plain Persian, 6–20 words}. Use [] if nothing needs it. Never invent etymology.
- "tags": 1 to 3 topic ids from this list that the text is truly about (use [] if none fits): sabr (صبر), shukr (شکر), tawakkul (توکل), omid (امید و ناامیدی), ghaza (رضا و قناعت), khashm (خشم), hasad (حسد), kibr (تکبر و تواضع), tama (طمع و حرص), ikhlas (اخلاص و ریا), khawf (ترس از خدا), zaban (زبان و سخن), dorugh (راستی و دروغ), gheybat (غیبت و عیب‌جویی), amanat (امانت و وفای به عهد), edalat (عدالت و ستم), hilm (بردباری و گذشت), hayaa (حیا و پاکدامنی), sakhavat (بخشش و بخل), khanevade (پدر و مادر), farzand (فرزند و تربیت), hamsar (همسر و خانه), rahem (خویشاوندان), doost (دوستی و همنشین), hamsaye (همسایه و مردم), yatim (نیازمند و یتیم), aql (عقل و اندیشه), elm (علم و دانا), mashverat (مشورت و تصمیم), tajrebe (عبرت و تجربه), dunya (دنیا), marg (مرگ و آخرت), rozi (روزی و کار), mal (مال و ثروت), vaqt (فرصت و عمر), belaa (سختی و بلا), gonah (گناه و توبه), doa (دعا), zekr (یاد خدا), namaz (نماز), taqva (تقوا), iman (ایمان و یقین), tawhid (شناخت خدا), rahmat (رحمت خدا).
